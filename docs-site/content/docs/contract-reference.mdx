This is the reference for the contract `snacc-v001.testnet` (Rust, `near-sdk` 5.29.1, in the [`contracts`](https://github.com/Snacc-near/contracts) repo, folder `snacc/`). Names are copied from `lib.rs`, `state.rs` and `events.rs`. NEAR **testnet only**. The marketplace has its own reference on the [Marketplace](/docs/marketplace) page.

How to read the tables:

- **JSON types.** Ids, epochs, counts and levels (`strain_id`, `epoch`, `expected_target_nonce`, `buy_amount`, `spores_to_burn`, `initial_spores` and so on) are `u64` or `u8` and must be sent as JSON **numbers**. The contract refuses a string there: a view call with `{"strain_id":"1"}` answers `invalid type: string "1", expected u64` (checked against the live contract on 2026-11-14 with a read-only view call). Amounts and balances (`U128`: `amount`, `initial_snacc_stake`, `snacc_catalyst_fee`, `from_index`) are **decimal strings**, because a JSON number loses precision above 2^53.
- **Deposit.** What you must attach. `none` means the method is not `#[payable]` or ignores a stray deposit, and the app sends 0. `fee` means attached NEAR equal to the argument `snacc_catalyst_fee`. `1 yocto` means exactly 1 yoctoNEAR.
- **Gas.** The prepaid gas the app attaches, in TGas, read from the frontend's `GAS` table. A dash means the app never sends that method. The only gas rule in the contract is the one on `nft_transfer_call`.
- **Who.** `owner` is the owner of the Snacc named in the call, not the contract owner.

## Hatch and account

| Method | Arguments | Deposit | Gas | Who | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `incubate_strain` | `role`, `initial_snacc_stake`, `initial_spores` | the stake | 100 | anyone | Returns the new `StrainVesselView`. `role` is `Extractor`, `Fermenter` or `Synthesizer`. With NEAR attached, `initial_snacc_stake` must be `"0"` or equal to the deposit, else `ERR_STAKE_DEPOSIT_MISMATCH`: 80% becomes the stomach and 20% the Pantry, and the stake is minted as new `$SNACC` supply (event `ft_mint`). With no NEAR attached, the stake is debited from the caller's `$SNACC` account balance instead. `initial_spores` is the number of `mt:spore-ignis` the new Snacc starts with: the contract applies no cap and no price to it, and the app sends 10 |
| `materialize_subaccount` | `strain_id` | at least 0.02 NEAR | - | owner | Creates the keyless sub-account `s-<id>-<tag>.snacc-v001.testnet` and locks the Snacc (`pending_callbacks`) until the callback runs. A failed creation refunds the deposit. `<tag>` is the block height of the last `new` or `migrate` |
| `storage_deposit` | `account_id?`, `registration_only?` | at least 5 milliNEAR for the first registration | 30 | anyone | `NEP-145`. Registers an account so it can receive `ft_transfer`. With `registration_only` the excess is refunded |
| `storage_withdraw` | `amount?` | none | - | registered | Withdraws the excess deposit. The 5 milliNEAR minimum stays locked while the account holds `$SNACC` |
| `storage_unregister` | `force?` | 1 yocto | - | registered | Removes the registration. With `$SNACC` still on the account it needs `force: true` |
| `storage_balance_of`, `storage_balance_bounds` | `account_id` / none | view | - | anyone | `StorageBalance { total, available }` or null; the bounds are min 5 milliNEAR and no max |

## Feeding and production

| Method | Arguments | Deposit | Gas | Who | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `feed_strain` | `strain_id`, `amount` | none | 100 | owner | Takes a fee of up to `amount` from the stomach (bursts, rescue feeding and dormancy can lower it) and runs it through the central bank, mints `$ATP`, sets vitality to effective vitality + 500. Returns the view. Event `fed`. Fails if starving |
| `allocate_atp` | `strain_id`, `mitokondria`, `nukleus`, `lisosom` | none | 50 | owner | Sets each organelle to the given **level** (0 to 5, `ERR_ORGANEL_LEVEL_MAX`). Costs 1 `$ATP` per level gained. Once per epoch (`ERR_ALREADY_ALLOCATED`) |
| `extract_spores` | `strain_id`, `spore_type`, `snacc_catalyst_fee` | fee | 100 | owner | Extractor role, 1 `$ATP`. `spore_type` is `mt:spore-ignis`, `mt:spore-cryo` or `mt:spore-lumin`. Mints 1 spore per call |
| `transmute_spores_to_enzyme` | `strain_id`, `spores_to_burn`, `snacc_catalyst_fee` | fee after the diet discount | 100 | owner | Fermenter role, 2 `$ATP`. `spores_to_burn` must be even and above 0 (`ERR_RECIPE_NOT_MET`). The attached deposit must equal the fee after the zone discount (`fee * (10000 - diet_bps) / 10000`) |
| `synthesize_elixir` | `strain_id`, `elixir_type`, `enzymes_to_burn`, `snacc_catalyst_fee` | fee | 100 | owner | Synthesizer role, 3 `$ATP`. `elixir_type` is `mt:elixir-homunculus`, `mt:elixir-homeostasis` or `mt:elixir-aether`. `enzymes_to_burn` must be a whole number of batches (3 for homunculus and aether, 2 for homeostasis). Aether needs a TEE `phenotype_trait` |

All three stage methods also fail with `ERR_STRAIN_STARVING`, `ERR_ROLE_NOT_ALLOWED` or `ERR_NOT_ENOUGH_ATP`, restore 500 vitality and restart the hunger clock. The catalyst fee is chosen by the caller, and the contract sets no minimum (the app defaults to 0.001 NEAR).

## Tokens: `NEP-141` and `NEP-171`

`$SNACC` has 24 decimals. `ft_balance_of` counts **account** balances only (bounties, payouts and dividends credited to an account). `$SNACC` inside a Snacc is not part of it. `get_total_wealth` adds both.

| Method | Arguments | Deposit | Gas | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `ft_transfer` | `receiver_id`, `amount`, `memo?` | 1 yocto | 30 | The receiver must be registered with `storage_deposit` (`ERR_ACCOUNT_NOT_REGISTERED`) |
| `ft_transfer_call` | `receiver_id`, `amount`, `memo?`, `msg` | 1 yocto | - | Transfers, then calls `ft_on_transfer` on the receiver (20 TGas) and refunds the unused part |
| `nft_transfer` | `receiver_id`, `token_id`, `approval_id?`, `memo?` | 1 yocto | 50 | Moves the whole Snacc: stomach, inventory, organelles, `$ATP`. `approval_id` is accepted and ignored. Dead Snaccs cannot move |
| `nft_transfer_call` | `receiver_id`, `token_id`, `approval_id?`, `memo?`, `msg` | 1 yocto | 100 for a listing | Needs more than 50 TGas prepaid (`ERR_MORE_GAS_REQUIRED`). Calls `nft_on_transfer`; a receiver that returns `true`, fails, or answers badly gives the token back |
| `ft_metadata`, `ft_balance_of`, `ft_total_supply` | none / `account_id` | view | - | `ft_metadata` has `decimals` 24 and symbol `$SNACC` |
| `nft_metadata`, `nft_total_supply`, `nft_token`, `nft_tokens`, `nft_supply_for_owner`, `nft_tokens_for_owner` | `token_id`, `from_index?`, `limit?`, `account_id` | view | - | `NEP-177` and `NEP-181`. The `token_id` is the `strain_id` as a string. `nft_token` returns `media` as a `data:image/svg+xml;base64,...` image drawn from the live state |

There is no `NEP-178` approval registry, and no public `mt_*` methods: molecules are internal.

## Events

Events are `NEP-297` log lines:

```text
EVENT_JSON:{"standard":"snacc","version":"1.0.0","event":"fed","data":[{ ... }]}
```

Standard `snacc`, version `1.0.0`. All amounts in the payloads are JSON numbers except where noted; read them with a big-number parser.

| --- | --- |
| `strain_created` | `strain_id`, `owner_id`, `role_mask`, `initial_snacc_stake`, `spores_minted` |

## Error codes

A failed call panics with one of these messages. The app turns them into plain-English text.

| Code | Raised when |
| --- | --- |
| `ERR_STRAIN_NOT_FOUND` | the strain id does not exist, or a token id is not a number |
| `ERR_NOT_OWNED_BY_CALLEE` | the caller does not own the Snacc (feed, divide, allocate, adopt and takeover raider, splice dominant, NPC, transfers, genome, `ft_burn`) |
| `ERR_NOT_STRAIN_OWNER` | the caller does not own the Snacc (stage actions, aether redeem, dividend claim) |
| `ERR_STAKE_DEPOSIT_MISMATCH` | the attached NEAR does not equal the stake or the catalyst fee |
| `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_DEPOSIT` | hatching with 0 NEAR and a 0 stake, `materialize_subaccount` below 0.02 NEAR, `storage_deposit` below 5 milliNEAR, `storage_unregister` with a balance and no `force` |
| `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` | not enough `$SNACC` or molecules, or `feed_strain` with an amount of 0 |
| `ERR_ROLE_MASK_NOT_SINGLE` | an internal check that a new Snacc has one role bit |
| `ERR_STRAIN_STARVING` | the action needs a Snacc with effective vitality above 0 |
| `ERR_ROLE_NOT_ALLOWED` | the Snacc lacks the role bit of that stage |
| `ERR_UNKNOWN_TOKEN` | an unknown `spore_type` or `elixir_type` |
| `ERR_RECIPE_NOT_MET` | the burn count is 0, odd (transmute) or not a whole number of batches (synthesize) |
| `ERR_REQUIRES_TEE_ATTESTATION` | aether without a TEE `phenotype_trait` |
| `ERR_NOT_ENOUGH_ATP` | the Snacc cannot pay the `$ATP` cost |
| `ERR_ALREADY_ALLOCATED` | `allocate_atp` twice in one epoch |
| `ERR_ORGANEL_LEVEL_MAX` | an organelle level above 5 |
| `ERR_SAME_STRAIN` | the same Snacc on both sides, or a transfer to yourself |
| `ERR_NOT_SAME_OWNER` | splice or conjugate with a Snacc you do not own |
| `ERR_STRAIN_ALREADY_SPLICED` | a dead Snacc (`Liquidated` or `SplicedBurned`) used in divide, splice, conjugate, allocate or as a target |
| `ERR_ALREADY_LIQUIDATED` | harvest, adopt or transfer of a dead Snacc |
| `ERR_COLONY_FULL` | the colony already has `MAX_COLONY_SIZE` (5) living Snaccs |
| `ERR_MAX_GENERATION` | the new generation would pass `MAX_GENERATION` (8) |
| `ERR_BIRTHS_EXCEEDED` | `total_divides` would exceed `total_mergers + total_lyses` |
| `ERR_NOT_DISTRESSED` | the target's effective vitality is not 1 to 2000 |
| `ERR_STRAIN_NOT_INSOLVENT` | harvest of a Snacc whose effective vitality is above 0 |
| `ERR_PENDING_ASYNC_CALLBACK_LOCKED` | the target has an unfinished callback |
| `ERR_STATE_NONCE_CHANGED_SELLER_DRAIN_BLOCKED` | `expected_target_nonce` does not match the target's `state_nonce` |
| `ERR_TRAIT_MISMATCH` | `conjugate_traits` with a trait that is not the source's current one |
| `ERR_NPC_UNKNOWN` | an unknown `npc_id` |
| `ERR_NPC_OUT_OF_STOCK` | the NPC has fewer spores than `buy_amount` |
| `ERR_BAD_AMOUNT` | `buy_amount` is 0, or `advance_sandbox_clock` hours outside 1 to 720 |
| `ERR_EPOCH_NOT_ENDED` | `advance_epoch` before the epoch's 24 hours are over |
| `ERR_EPOCH_NOT_CLOSED` | a dividend claim for an epoch that is not closed |
| `ERR_DIVIDEND_CLAIM_EXPIRED` | a dividend claim more than 8 epochs back |
| `ERR_DIVIDEND_ALREADY_CLAIMED` | a second claim for the same epoch and Snacc |
| `ERR_DIVIDEND_POOL_EMPTY` | the payout would be 0 |
| `ERR_INSUFFICIENT_AETHER` | `amount` below 1, or more aether than the Snacc holds |
| `ERR_ROLE_LOCKED_NEEDS_SYNTHESIZER_GENE` | aether redeem on a Snacc without the Synthesizer bit |
| `ERR_COMMITMENT_ALREADY_PENDING` | a second `commit_genome_experiment` before an apply |
| `ERR_COMMITMENT_MISMATCH` | the apply hash is not the pending commitment |
| `ERR_TEE_RESPONSE_TOO_LARGE` | the response body is over 4096 bytes |
| `ERR_SIGNER_NOT_REGISTERED` | the signer key is not in the registry |
| `ERR_TEE_RESPONSE_HASH_OR_ED25519_MISMATCH` | the signature is invalid, or the body is not the expected JSON |
| `ERR_TEE_REPLAY` | the `chat_id` was already used |
| `ERR_PAYLOAD_STRAIN_MISMATCH` | the `strain_id` inside the answer is not the one in the call |
| `ERR_GUARDRAIL_SHIFT` | `efficiency_shift_bps` outside -1500 to 1500 |
| `ERR_GUARDRAIL_VITALITY` | `vitality_bonus` above 500 |
| `ERR_TEE_TRAIT_INVALID` | `phenotype_trait` longer than 32 characters |
| `ERR_BAD_HEX`, `ERR_BAD_SIGNATURE_LEN` | a hex argument of the wrong length or with bad characters |
| `ERR_NOT_CONTRACT_OWNER` | a contract-owner method called by someone else |
| `ERR_TEE_SIGNER_OVERWRITE`, `ERR_TEE_SIGNER_NOT_FOUND` | signer registry rules |
| `ERR_SANDBOX_CLOCK_DISABLED` | `advance_sandbox_clock` on an account that does not end in `.testnet` |
| `ERR_ACCOUNT_NOT_REGISTERED` | an `ft_transfer` receiver, or a `storage_withdraw` caller, that is not registered |
| `ERR_YOCTO_REQUIRED` | a transfer or `storage_unregister` without exactly 1 yoctoNEAR |
| `ERR_MORE_GAS_REQUIRED` | `nft_transfer_call` with 50 TGas or less prepaid |

`ERR_STRAIN_ALREADY_INCUBATED` is defined in the source but never raised.
