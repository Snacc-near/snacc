## The basics

**What is Snacc?**
NFTs that eat a memecoin. Each Snacc is a living NFT with its own stomach of `$SNACC`, an inventory of molecules, `$ATP` energy and a job in a small supply chain. Feed it or it gets weak and others can take it over. See the [Introduction](/docs) and [The Snacc organism](/docs/organism).

**Is this real money, or a way to make money?**
No. Everything runs on NEAR **testnet**. `$SNACC`, `$ATP` and testnet NEAR have no monetary value. The price swings come from the game's central bank and are a feature of the game, not a promise. The dividend pool redistributes other players' fees and guarantees nothing. See [Security and honesty](/docs/security).

**Is it live on mainnet?**
No. Nothing is deployed on mainnet. The [Roadmap](/docs/roadmap) lists plans, and plans are not promises.

**Where is the code?**
In the public [`Snacc-near`](https://github.com/Snacc-near) organisation: `contracts`, `sdk`, `backend`, `frontend`, `oracle-tee`, `scripts`, and the umbrella `Snacc` repo with the README, the evidence and this docs site. See [Architecture](/docs/architecture).

## Playing

**Do I need a wallet?**
Not to look around. The demo mode needs nothing, and the live read mode reads `snacc-v001.testnet` without a wallet. To hatch, feed and divide you need a NEAR testnet wallet (or an email login) with testnet NEAR from the [faucet](https://docs.near.org/getting-started/faucet). See [Play in 60 seconds](/docs/play).

**Is there a free starter pack?**
Not yet. The `starter-pack` function is deployed but not enabled, because its faucet secret is not set. Use the faucet linked above.

**Can I hatch without NEAR?**
Hatching attaches NEAR (the stake), so it always opens the wallet. Hatching without any NEAR, through a relayer or meta-transactions, is a plan for after the hackathon and is not built. A hatch can also be paid from `$SNACC` in your account balance, if you have some.

**What happens to my hatching stake?**
80% becomes the Snacc's stomach and 20% goes to the Pantry, the rule-bound treasury.

**How often should I feed?**
About once a day. With no feeding and no lysosome, an Extractor becomes Distressed after about 65 hours and starving after about 85, a Fermenter after 55 and 71, and a Synthesizer after 44 and 57. Feeding and every stage action add 500 vitality and restart the clock. See [The Snacc organism](/docs/organism).

**What if I forget?**
A Distressed Snacc (effective vitality 1 to 2000) can be adopted or taken over by another player. A starving one (0) can be harvested by anyone: 10% of its stomach goes to the harvester and 90% to the Pantry. A harvested Snacc is dead and cannot be revived or transferred. See [Colony actions](/docs/colony).

**Why did my divide fail?**
Divide has three fences: at most 5 living Snaccs (`ERR_COLONY_FULL`), generation 8 at most (`ERR_MAX_GENERATION`), and a global gate that lets divisions run only on a budget created by mergers and harvests (`ERR_BIRTHS_EXCEEDED`). It also costs 3 `$ATP`.

**Is `$ATP` a token?**
No. It is the Snacc's internal energy, kept in the field `atp_balance`. It has no supply and cannot be transferred.

**Can `$SNACC` be minted?**
Only from a real NEAR stake at hatching. Everything else moves or burns existing `$SNACC`.

**What are the "mK" numbers?**
`CrucibleTemp`, the central bank's reading, in millikelvin. Above 610 a slice of each fee is burned, below 600 the Pantry pays a rebate. See [CrucibleTemp](/docs/crucible-temp).

**Why does the app show a dash?**
Where the chain gives no value, the app shows `—`. It never invents a number.

## The AI and the NPCs

**Does an AI decide who wins?**
No. A model in the NEAR AI Cloud TEE scores one formula, and the contract applies the result only inside fixed limits (plus or minus 15% efficiency, at most 500 vitality) and only if the enclave's signature checks out on chain. The NPC merchants are fixed formulas, not AI. See [TEE verification](/docs/tee) and [The NPC merchants](/docs/npcs).

**Is my formula private?**
Identities (accounts, emails, keys) are removed, and raw dosages are hidden behind a hash and normalized ratios before the enclave sees the text. The formula text is not written on chain, only a hash of the request is. The enclave's answer (the numbers and the trait) is public in the apply transaction. See [TEE verification](/docs/tee).

## Trust and safety

**Is one-tap play safe?**
It adds one function-call key that can only call `snacc-v001.testnet`, only eight deposit-free methods, with a 0.25 NEAR gas allowance. It cannot attach NEAR or touch your other funds. It has **not** been verified with a real wallet yet. See [One-tap play](/docs/one-tap).

**What is the sandbox clock?**
An owner-only function that moves contract time forward on `.testnet` accounts, so Distressed and starving windows and epoch closes can be shown without waiting. The evidence says where it was used. It does not exist on a mainnet account.

**Which parts are verified on chain?**
The contract's rules, through the transactions on the [Evidence](/docs/evidence) page. Not verified: the app's wallet flow, one-tap play, the Market window, push delivery and more than one dividend epoch.

**Where do I report a problem?**
Use GitHub, in the repo of the [`Snacc-near`](https://github.com/Snacc-near) organisation that holds the affected code.
