## The stomach

Every Snacc owns a `$SNACC` balance of its own. It is a separate ledger from your wallet balance: the NEP-141 view `ft_balance_of` counts **account** balances only, and `$SNACC` inside a Snacc moves only through game rules.

- At hatching, 80% of the stake becomes the stomach and 20% goes to the Pantry.
- `feed_strain` burns a slice of the stomach as food. The fee then runs through the central bank (`CrucibleTemp`): in a Fever zone part of it is burned, and in a Hypothermia zone the Snacc may get a rebate from the Pantry.
- The stomach travels with the NFT. `nft_transfer` moves the whole cell: stomach, inventory, organelles, `$ATP` and `state_nonce`, because all of it is keyed by `strain_id`. Dead cells (`Liquidated` or `SplicedBurned`) cannot be transferred.
- `state_nonce` rises on every balance or inventory change. `adopt` and `hostile_takeover_splice` take the nonce you saw (`expected_target_nonce`) and fail if the Snacc changed in between, and a `pending_callbacks` lock blocks changes while its sub-account is being created.
- `$SNACC` is born only from a real NEAR stake at hatching. Everything else moves existing balances or burns them.

## The inventory

The inventory holds **nine molecules** in three stages. Each Snacc has a job (its role) that decides which stage it can work in. The contract keeps the inventory as `NEP-245`-style accounting: `mt:*` token ids, `mt_burn` of inputs and `mt_mint` of outputs. It does **not** expose public `mt_*` methods, so molecules are contract-internal fuel, not standalone transferable tokens. Read a balance with the view `get_inventory(strain_id, token_id)`.

Every stage also pays a `$SNACC` catalyst fee that flows into the central bank, and every successful stage action restores 500 vitality and restarts the hunger clock.

What the elixirs do on chain today:

- **`mt:elixir-homeostasis`**: a passive shield. A Snacc holding at least one takes only 50% of the Fever burn on its fees. It is not consumed.
- **`mt:elixir-aether`**: burned in `synthesize_and_redeem_aether` to claim a share of the industrial dividend pool.
- **`mt:elixir-homunculus`**: made and held. The contract has no method that consumes it yet.

### Roles

A new Snacc has exactly one role bit, chosen at hatching. The role sets the hunger rate and the stage it can work in. Merging two Snaccs (`splice_strains`) combines their bits.

| Role | `role_mask` | `bmr_per_hour` | Works in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Extractor | `0b001` | 100 | Stage 1 |
| Fermenter | `0b010` | 120 | Stage 2 |
| Synthesizer | `0b100` | 150 | Stage 3 |

## Metabolism: BMR and vitality

Vitality is a number from 0 to 10000 (100.00%). A new Snacc starts at **8500**. The basal metabolic rate (`bmr_per_hour`) is how much vitality it loses every hour.

The contract never runs a timer. It works the current value out whenever someone reads or touches the Snacc ("lazy BMR"):

```text
effective_vitality = vitality - floor(hours since last_fed_sec) * bmr_effective
bmr_effective      = bmr_per_hour * (10000 - 1000 * lisosom) / 10000
```

`feed_strain` and every stage action set `vitality` to `min(10000, effective_vitality + 500)` and reset `last_fed_sec`.

How long a Snacc lasts with no feeding and no lysosome (derived from the constants above):

| Role | Distressed after | Starving after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extractor | 65 hours | 85 hours |
| Fermenter | 55 hours | 71 hours |
| Synthesizer | 44 hours | 57 hours |

| Tier (`SolvencyTier`) | Effective vitality | What is open to others |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Tier1Healthy` | above 2000 (above 20%) | Nothing: other players cannot touch it. Its owner can merge it with another of their Snaccs (`splice_strains`) |
| `Tier2Chapter11Distressed` | 1 to 2000 | `adopt`: the adopter pays book value (the stomach plus the inventory at 1 `$SNACC` per molecule unit) to the old owner and takes the Snacc over as it is. Or `hostile_takeover_splice`: pay book value plus 15% and absorb it into one of your Snaccs |
| `Tier3Chapter7Insolvent` | 0 | `harvest_starving_strain` by anyone: 10% of the stomach to the caller, 90% to the Pantry. The Snacc can no longer be fed |

The stored `status` of a Snacc is one of `Active`, `Distressed`, `Liquidated` or `SplicedBurned`. A starving Snacc stays `Active` until someone harvests it. Use `get_solvency_tier` to read the tier.

## `$ATP`: the energy

`$ATP` is the Snacc's internal energy, kept in the field `atp_balance`.

- It is **not a token**: it has no total supply and cannot be transferred.
- A new Snacc starts with 50. A divided child also starts with 50.
- `feed_strain` mints it from the fee actually paid: `atp = fee / 10^15 * efficiency_bps / 10000 * (10000 + 1000 * mitokondria) / 10000`.
- It pays for actions:

| Action | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| `extract_spores` | 1 |
| `transmute_spores_to_enzyme` | 2 |
| `synthesize_elixir` | 3 |
| `divide_strain` | 3 |
| `conjugate_traits` | 2 |
| `splice_strains` | 5 |
| `hostile_takeover_splice` | 10 |
| `allocate_atp` | 1 per level gained |

If a Snacc has too little, the call fails with `ERR_NOT_ENOUGH_ATP`.

## Organelles

`allocate_atp(strain_id, mitokondria, nukleus, lisosom)` spends `$ATP` to set the levels of three organelle slots, field `organel`. Each level costs 1 `$ATP` to gain. The maximum is level 5, and you can allocate once per epoch (`EPOCH_DURATION_SEC` = 86400, 24 hours). A second try in the same epoch fails with `ERR_ALREADY_ALLOCATED`, and going past 5 fails with `ERR_ORGANEL_LEVEL_MAX`.

| Slot | Effect per level | Where it applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `mitokondria` | +10% `$ATP` minted | `feed_strain` |
| `nukleus` | +10% production output | `transmute_spores_to_enzyme` and `synthesize_elixir` |
| `lisosom` | -10% effective BMR | the hunger drain |

Organelles are inherited when two Snaccs merge (the higher level of each slot wins) and are halved between parent and child when a Snacc divides. Only these three slots are built. Other organelles you may read about in the design notes are roadmap ideas, not code.
