**Snacc** (`$SNACC`) is NFTs that eat a memecoin. *Feed your Snacc or it gets snacked.*

Each Snacc is a living NFT. It holds its own `$SNACC` in a stomach, keeps an inventory of molecules, burns energy every hour and works in one stage of a small supply chain. If you forget to feed it, it gets weak, other players can adopt or take it over, and a starved Snacc can be harvested by anyone.

<Callout type="warn" title="Testnet only">
Everything on this site describes NEAR **testnet** (`snacc-v001.testnet`). `$SNACC` on testnet has no monetary value, and volatility is a game feature, not a promise. A feature counts as live only where the [status table in the README](https://github.com/Snacc-near/Snacc#whats-in-the-app) says so. Plans are marked as plans.
</Callout>

## What a Snacc is

A Snacc is one organism and one NFT (`NEP-171`, with a live on-chain SVG through `NEP-177`):

- **A stomach.** A balance of `$SNACC` that belongs to the Snacc itself, not to your wallet. Feeding burns some of it.
- **An inventory.** Nine molecules in three stages: spores, enzymes and elixirs.
- **A metabolism.** A basal metabolic rate (`BMR`) drains *vitality* every hour. Feeding or working refills it.
- **Energy.** `$ATP`, an internal unit that pays for actions. It is not a token and cannot be transferred.
- **A job.** Extractor, Fermenter or Synthesizer, one stage of the supply chain.

The whole game runs inside **one contract**, `snacc-v001.testnet`. See [The Snacc organism](/docs/organism) for the parts and [Architecture](/docs/architecture) for how the contract, the indexer, Supabase and the TEE fit together.


| Layer | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Living NFT** | The organism: stomach, inventory, vitality and BMR, and an SVG the contract draws from its own state. |
| **Supply chain and corporates** | Three stages of `NEP-245`-style molecules, three job roles that can be merged (`splice_strains`), taken over while distressed (`hostile_takeover_splice`, `adopt`) or harvested once starved (`harvest_starving_strain`). A player can also grow a colony of up to 5 with `divide_strain`. |
| **Secret genome** | A formula is evaluated by a model inside the `NEAR AI Cloud TEE`. The contract checks the enclave signature itself with `env::ed25519_verify` and clamps the result to ±1500 bps. |
| **Central bank** | `CrucibleTemp` reads the colony's activity. Above 610 mK (Fever) a slice of each fee is burned; below 600 mK (Hypothermia) the Pantry gives a rebate. No admin can withdraw the Pantry. |

Three autonomous NPC merchants (`npc-extractor`, `npc-central-arb`, `npc-vulture-ma`) quote and sell spores from inside the contract, so the economy has a counterparty even with one player.

## Words you will see

| In the game | In the contract | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Snacc, strain, cell | `StrainVessel`, `strain_id` | One organism and its NFT |
| Fullness, vitality | `vitality`, `effective_vitality` | Health on a 0 to 10000 scale (100.00%) |
| Hunger rate | `bmr_per_hour` | Vitality lost per hour: 100, 120 or 150 by role |
| The Pantry | treasury | The rule-bound treasury. It pays rebates and receives a share of fees |
| Appetite meter | `CrucibleTemp`, `get_appetite_meter` | The colony's activity reading, in mK |
| Feeding Frenzy, Famine Relief | Fever, Hypothermia | The two zones that burn or rebate |
| Snack it | `harvest_starving_strain` | Harvest a starved Snacc: 10% to you, 90% to the Pantry |

## What is live today

The table follows the README of the [`Snacc` umbrella repo](https://github.com/Snacc-near/Snacc), checked 2026-11-14. "Live" means present in the production build; it is not the same as "tried on chain with a real wallet".

| Part | Status |
| --- | --- |
| Landing page and the game at [`/app`](https://snacc-nu.vercel.app/app), with the `demo · simulated` mode | Live |
| `live testnet` mode: strains, macro state, Great Feast and NPC quotes from contract views and the read-only backend | Live, reads only |
| Wallet login (NEAR Connect) and signed game actions | Live. The UI wallet flow has **not** been exercised on chain yet |
| Hatch, feed, allocate ATP and divide payloads | Accepted by the contract on testnet, signed with the CLI, not through the UI wallet |
| One-tap play (restricted access key, deposit-free actions only) | Live, opt-in. **Not verified** on testnet |
| Market window for `snacc-market.testnet` | Live. The contract was exercised with CLI-signed transactions; the UI market with a real wallet is not verified |
| Profiles, quests, leaderboards on Supabase | Live, off-chain data |
| Testnet starter pack, account deletion | **Not enabled** / **not live** yet |

Planned and not built: hatching without any NEAR (a relayer or meta-transactions) is post-hackathon work.
