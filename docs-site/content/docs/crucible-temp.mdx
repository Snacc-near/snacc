`CrucibleTemp` is the reactive central bank of Snacc. It is not a person or a committee. It is a number the contract works out from how busy the colony is, and two rules that act on every fee. When the colony is hot, part of each fee is **burned**. When the colony is cold, the **Pantry** pays a small rebate. `$SNACC` on testnet has no monetary value, and the swings are a game feature, not a promise.

## The temperature

The contract keeps a running activity volume, `volume_epoch`, and reads the temperature in millikelvin (mK):

```text
CrucibleTemp = floor(1000 * V_decayed / max(total_snacc_supply, 1))
```

`V_decayed` is the volume minus a slow decay, computed lazily whenever someone reads it, so no keeper or timer is needed. Anyone can read it with the view `get_crucible_temp`, or with `get_appetite_meter` for the zone, the burst and the Great Feast together.

What feeds the volume (all amounts are in yocto units):

- **Fees.** Every fee that enters the thermostat adds its amount: the catalyst fee on `extract_spores`, `transmute_spores_to_enzyme` and `synthesize_elixir`, and the fee `feed_strain` takes from a stomach. The catalyst fee is **attached NEAR** that must equal the argument `snacc_catalyst_fee`. The caller picks the amount: the contract sets no minimum, and the app's default is 0.001 NEAR. The contract books the amount one to one in its own `$SNACC` accounts, and the solvency view counts it as an outside inflow (`total_catalyst_fees`).
- **Molecule work.** Each molecule unit burned or minted in a stage action adds `units * VOLUME_SCALE * P_BASE`, which is `units * 5 * 100`.
- **ATP.** `feed_strain` adds `atp_minted * ATP_VOLUME_SCALE`, with `ATP_VOLUME_SCALE = 600000000000000`. So part of the temperature reading is made of `$ATP` that feeding minted.
- **Harvest cash.** The bounty and the Pantry share of a harvest count as volume.

Rebates, dividends, transfers and the hatching stake are left out on purpose, so the bank cannot feed itself.

A new Snacc adds `$SNACC` supply but no volume, so hatching **cools** the colony. Fees heat it.

## The three zones

| Zone | `crucible_temp` | Contract string | What happens to a fee |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fever | above 610 mK | `FEVER` | 3.5% of the fee is burned (`FEVER_BURN_BPS = 350`) |
| Normal | 600 to 610 mK | `NORMAL` | no burn and no rebate |
| Hypothermia | below 600 mK | `HYPO` | the Pantry pays a 2% rebate (`REBATE_BPS = 200`) |

The game calls Fever the "Feeding Frenzy" and Hypothermia "Famine Relief". The thresholds follow the code: **610 mK** for Fever, not the 1400 mK of older design notes (1400 was unreachable, so the burn never ran).

Whenever a fee moves the colony into another zone, the contract emits `zone_changed` with `from_zone`, `to_zone` and `crucible_temp`. The zone is worked out from the same thresholds everywhere, so the NFT image, the views and the events always agree.

## What happens to a fee

Every fee goes through the same path. The fee is the amount charged by the action, not the whole stake.

```pele
  ADD --> ZZ{"Zone"}

  REB --> DONE
```

The numbers, from the code:

- **The burn.** In Fever, `fee * 350 / 10000` is removed from `total_snacc_supply` and added to `total_snacc_burned`. A Snacc that holds at least one `mt:elixir-homeostasis` pays only half of that burn. The elixir is not used up.
- **The split.** After any burn, 50% of the rest (`DIVIDEND_INFLOW_BPS = 5000`) goes to the dividend inflow of the running epoch, and 50% to the Pantry. See [The Great Feast and the Industrial Dividend Pool](/docs/feast-dividend).
- **The rebate.** In Hypothermia the Pantry pays `fee * 200 / 10000` into the stomach of the Snacc that paid. The total rebate in one epoch is capped at 5% of the Pantry balance taken at the start of the epoch (`MAX_EPOCH_TREASURY_DRAIN_BPS = 500`), and never more than the Pantry holds. There is no admin function that withdraws from the Pantry.

## Other things the zone changes

- **Diet discount.** In `transmute_spores_to_enzyme`, a Snacc with the Extractor bit pays a fee 20% lower in Fever, and a Snacc with the Synthesizer bit pays 20% lower in Hypothermia. The event `transmuted` carries `diet_bps`.
- **Nutrient Burst.** A harvest sets a discount on `feed_strain` for one epoch: `200 bps` plus the harvested share of the supply, at most `2000 bps`. The discount is paid from the same 5% Pantry quota. `get_burst_state` returns the active discount and its end epoch.
- **Rescue feeding.** Feeding a weak Snacc is cheaper: the fee multiplier rises linearly from 20% at vitality 0 to 100% at vitality 10000.
- **Dormancy.** When the decayed volume is below 1% of the supply, the feeding fee drops to 50 bps of its value, so a quiet colony is cheap to keep alive.
- **Risk heat.** `get_risk_heat` is a 0 to 10000 "betting intensity": the temperature as a share of the Fever threshold, plus 1000 while a burst is active and 1000 while the dividend pool holds at least 1% of the supply.

## Honest limits

<Callout type="warn" title="Read the temperature with the scale in mind">
The decay is `VOLUME_DECAY_PER_SEC = 50`, in the same yocto units as the volume. 50 yocto per second is about 4.3 million yocto per day, roughly 4 x 10^-18 NEAR. Against volumes of 10^22 to 10^24 yocto, the decay barely moves the temperature today. In practice the reading changes when fees arrive and when hatching raises the supply.
</Callout>

- The 610 and 600 mK thresholds come from a calibration of the economic simulation. In the project's simulation of 10,000 epochs the colony spent about 5.9% of the time in Fever, 93% in Hypothermia and 1.2% in Normal. That is a model result, not live data.
- On testnet the scale is tiny. The one live Fever firing (the `zone_changed` from `HYPO` to `FEVER` at 916 mK, with the burn halved by a homeostasis elixir) was caused by a catalyst fee that the contract account paid to itself, so it moved no value between players. See [Evidence](/docs/evidence).
- The read-only backend labels the middle zone `Goldilocks` in `/api/crucible/macro`, while the contract calls it `NORMAL`. They are the same zone. The `ruleBoundTreasury` field there is the Pantry balance snapshot taken at the start of the epoch, not a live balance.
- This is a thermostat, not a price peg. It does not guarantee any value for `$SNACC`.
