Snacc has three non-player merchants. They are records inside the contract (`snacc-v001.testnet`), so a single player always has someone to trade with. NEAR **testnet only**.

| `npc_id` | Bio-Lab name | Target spore stock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `npc-extractor` | Pyre-X | 500 |
| `npc-central-arb` | Myco-7 | 1000 |
| `npc-vulture-ma` | Void-0 | 300 |

Each NPC starts with its target stock of `mt:spore-ignis`, an empty `$SNACC` reserve and a base price of 100. All three use the same price formula and differ only in the target stock and the name.

<Callout type="info" title="Rules, not AI">
"Autonomous" here means that nobody operates them. An NPC is a state record and a fixed formula. It is not a language model, and it does not decide anything. The "reasoning tooltip" in the quote is a text template filled with the current numbers.
</Callout>

## The scarcity price

An NPC prices the next sale by how empty its shelf would be afterwards:

```text
stock_after = stock - buy_amount
unit_price  = max(1, floor(base_price * target_stock / max(stock_after, 1)))
total_cost  = unit_price * buy_amount
```

The fewer spores the NPC would have left, the higher the unit price. At the target stock the price is the base price, 100. For `npc-extractor` (target 500, stock 500):

| `buy_amount` | Stock after | Unit price | Total cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 499 | 100 | 100 |
| 50 | 450 | 111 | 5,550 |
| 100 | 400 | 125 | 12,500 |
| 250 | 250 | 200 | 50,000 |
| 400 | 100 | 500 | 200,000 |

The 50 spore row matches the purchase run on testnet (unit price 111, total 5550). The unit price applies to **all** units of one purchase, based on the stock after the whole purchase.

Prices are in the smallest `$SNACC` unit. The token has 24 decimals, so a price of 100 is 10^-22 `$SNACC`. At that scale a purchase costs almost nothing, and the curve is a mechanic to see, not a cost to plan around.

## Quote and buy

Two methods:

- **`npc_quote_spores(npc_id, buy_amount)`** is a view. It returns the unit price, the total cost, the stock after the purchase, the current `crucible_temp_mk` and the `reasoning_tooltip`. `get_npc(npc_id)` returns the NPC record: `snacc_reserve`, `spores_stock`, `target_spores_stock`, `base_spore_price`, `trades_executed`.
- **`npc_buy_spores(strain_id, npc_id, buy_amount)`** buys `mt:spore-ignis` and pays from the Snacc's own stomach. You must own the Snacc. It needs no attached NEAR and no `$ATP`, so it is one of the eight deposit-free actions that one-tap play can send (see [One-tap play](/docs/one-tap)).

Why buy spores? A Fermenter needs `mt:spore-ignis` to make `mt:enzyme-amalgam`, the most used enzyme. An Extractor makes 1 spore per `extract_spores` call and pays `$ATP` and a catalyst fee for each, while an NPC sells a batch in one deposit-free call.

## What the NPCs do not do

- **They only sell, and only ignis spores.** There is no method to sell to an NPC, and the vault's idea of two-way liquidity is not built.
- **They never restock.** The stock only goes down, and no method refills it. A sold-out NPC answers `ERR_NPC_OUT_OF_STOCK`.
- **They keep what they earn.** The `snacc_reserve` only grows. No method pays it out. It is counted in the solvency view `get_global_solvency` as `sum_npc_reserve`.
- **They do not trade on their own.** An NPC acts only when a player calls `npc_buy_spores`. The `wallet_account` field is a label, not an account that signs anything.
