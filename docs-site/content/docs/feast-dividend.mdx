Two features run on the same clock, the **epoch**. An epoch is 24 hours of contract time (`EPOCH_DURATION_SEC = 86400`). The **Great Feast** grades how much the whole colony fed during an epoch. The **Industrial Dividend Pool** collects a share of every fee and lets Snaccs that burned `mt:elixir-aether` claim from it afterwards. Neither one promises a payout. `$SNACC` on testnet has no monetary value.

## The epoch clock

Nothing runs on a timer. Anyone can call `advance_epoch()` once the running epoch is over (`ERR_EPOCH_NOT_ENDED` before that). It closes the epoch, takes the dividend snapshot, grades the feast and opens the next one.

## The Great Feast

Every successful `feed_strain` adds its **effective fee** to the epoch's feeding volume. At the end of the epoch the contract compares that volume with a target:

- the target is 5% of the total `$SNACC` supply (`FEAST_TARGET_BPS = 500`);
- the "Goldilocks" band is plus or minus 20% of the target (`FEAST_BAND_BPS = 2000`).

| Verdict | When |
| --- | --- |
| `GOLDILOCKS` | the volume is inside the band |
| `OVERFED` | the volume is above the band |
| `UNDERFED` | the volume is below the band |

The verdict comes out in the event `feast_result` (`epoch`, `volume`, `target`, `verdict`). While the epoch runs, `get_appetite_meter` shows `feast_volume` against `feast_target`. The contract also keeps a **top 3 feeder** leaderboard per epoch by account (`get_top_feeder(epoch)`), and the app shows it on the Great Feast board.

<Callout type="warn" title="The feast pays nothing on chain today">
In the current contract the Great Feast only grades the epoch and keeps the leaderboard. It does not pay a bonus or a prize. Token bonuses for a Goldilocks epoch are a design idea, not built. The one epoch closed on testnet graded `UNDERFED`.
</Callout>

## The Industrial Dividend Pool

### Where the money comes from

Every fee that goes through the central bank (see [CrucibleTemp](/docs/crucible-temp)) is split. After any Fever burn, **50%** (`DIVIDEND_INFLOW_BPS = 5000`) goes to the running epoch's **dividend inflow** and 50% goes to the Pantry. The event `dividend_inflow` records each split. When the epoch closes, its inflow is added to the `industrial_dividend_pool`.

The pool only ever moves existing `$SNACC`. A claim does not mint anything, and a payout can never be larger than the pool balance.

### Two steps: burn, then claim

Paying the first player who shows up would let one player drain a whole epoch. So the claim is **pro-rata and in two steps**:

1. **Burn during the epoch.** `synthesize_and_redeem_aether(strain_id, amount)` burns `mt:elixir-aether` and records your contribution for the running epoch. You must own the Snacc, it needs the Synthesizer role bit (`ERR_ROLE_LOCKED_NEEDS_SYNTHESIZER_GENE`), it must not be starving, and you must hold at least `amount` aether (`ERR_INSUFFICIENT_AETHER`). The event is `aether_burned_for_dividend`. Nothing is paid yet.
2. **Claim after the epoch closes.** `claim_industrial_dividend(strain_id, epoch)` pays:

```text
payout = contribution * epoch_inflow / max(total_aether_burned_in_epoch, 1)
```

The payout goes into the Snacc's **stomach**, not into a wallet. It is capped at the pool balance (`ERR_DIVIDEND_POOL_EMPTY` if there is nothing to pay).

### The claim rules

| Rule | Error |
| --- | --- |
| The epoch must be closed (`epoch < last_closed_epoch`) | `ERR_EPOCH_NOT_CLOSED` |
| At most 8 epochs back (`MAX_DIVIDEND_CLAIM_EPOCHS = 8`) | `ERR_DIVIDEND_CLAIM_EXPIRED` |
| Once per epoch and strain | `ERR_DIVIDEND_ALREADY_CLAIMED` |
| Only the Snacc's owner | `ERR_NOT_STRAIN_OWNER` |
| The pool must hold a payout above 0 | `ERR_DIVIDEND_POOL_EMPTY` |

Read the state with `get_dividend_pool()` (pool, running inflow, last closed epoch), `get_epoch_dividend_rate(epoch)` and `get_dividend_contribution(epoch, strain_id)`, which returns your burned amount and whether it was claimed.

### Getting aether

`mt:elixir-aether` is the rarest molecule. `synthesize_elixir` makes it from 2 `mt:enzyme-amalgam` and 1 `mt:enzyme-synthase` per batch, needs the Synthesizer role, costs 3 `$ATP`, and works **only after a successful TEE mutation** of that Snacc (otherwise `ERR_REQUIRES_TEE_ATTESTATION`). See [TEE verification](/docs/tee) and [The Snacc organism](/docs/organism).

## No guarantee

<Callout type="warn" title="Work and redistribution, not income">
The pool pays out only the fees that other players' actions produced, only to Snaccs that burned aether in that epoch, and only in `$SNACC` that has no monetary value on testnet. A quiet epoch has a small inflow. A sole contributor takes the whole inflow of an epoch, and a crowded epoch is shared. Nobody can predict the amount. Aether costs real work: a TEE mutation, enzymes, `$ATP` and fees. Profit here means work plus redistribution, never a promise.
</Callout>

What was verified on testnet: one epoch closed, and one Snacc burned aether and claimed from it. That run had a single contributor, so it shows the mechanics and not a return. More than one epoch of dividends, claim expiry and several contributors are covered by unit tests only.
