`snacc-market.testnet` is a small marketplace for Snacc NFTs. A seller sends a Snacc to the market, the market holds it in **escrow**, and a buyer pays NEAR to get it. The contract is in the `market/` crate of the [`contracts`](https://github.com/Snacc-near/contracts) repo. It is **testnet only**: testnet NEAR and testnet `$SNACC` have no monetary value, the fee is 0%, and there is no promise of profit.

Because a Snacc carries its stomach, inventory, organelles and `$ATP` (see [The Snacc organism](/docs/organism)), selling the NFT sells the whole organism, including the `$SNACC` in its stomach.

## How a listing works

The market uses the standard `NEP-171` flow. The Snacc contract was given `nft_transfer_call` for this, and it calls the market's `nft_on_transfer`.

```pele
sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  actor S as Seller
  actor B as Buyer
  participant C as snacc-v001.testnet
  participant M as snacc-market.testnet
  S->>C: nft_transfer_call(market, token_id, msg with price), 1 yoctoNEAR
  C->>M: nft_on_transfer(sender_id, previous_owner_id, token_id, msg)
  M-->>C: false, the token stays in escrow
  Note over M: listed event, the listing is recorded
  alt the seller changes their mind
    S->>M: delist(token_id), 1 yoctoNEAR
    M->>C: nft_transfer back to the seller
    Note over M: delisted event
  else a buyer pays
    B->>M: buy(token_id) with at least the price attached
    M->>C: nft_transfer to the buyer
    M-->>S: price in NEAR
    M-->>B: the excess is refunded
    Note over M: sold event
  end
```

- **List.** The seller calls `nft_transfer_call` on the Snacc contract with the market as `receiver_id`, 1 yoctoNEAR attached, and the message `{"price":"<yoctoNEAR as a decimal string>"}`. The price must be above 0 and at most 10,000 NEAR. Unknown fields in the message are rejected. When anything is wrong, the market returns `true` and the Snacc goes straight back to the seller. Sellers attach nothing for storage: the market pays for each listing from its own balance.
- **Delist.** `delist(token_id)` by the seller, 1 yoctoNEAR. The Snacc goes back.
- **Buy.** `buy(token_id)` by anyone except the seller, attaching at least the price. The listing is removed first, then the Snacc is transferred, then the seller is paid the price and the buyer gets the excess back.
- **Change the price.** `update_price(token_id, price)` by the seller, 1 yoctoNEAR.

Limits: 20 open listings per seller and 1,000 in total. The owner of the market contract can only pause **new** listings (`set_listings_paused`). `buy`, `delist` and `update_price` keep working, and nobody can withdraw a listed token.

## When a purchase cannot settle

Escrow can fail in the middle, for example when a listed Snacc dies or is adopted while it sits in the market. The market handles it without losing money:

{/* Diagram checked against: contracts market/README.md ("Buy and failure handling", "Known limits"), market/src/lib.rs on_buy_settled, on_owner_checked. */}

```pele
flowchart TB
  B(["buy(token_id) with a deposit"]) --> R["Remove the listing, send nft_transfer to the buyer"]
  R --> Q{"Transfer succeeded?"}
  Q -->|"yes"| OK["Seller is paid the price,<br/>buyer gets the excess back,<br/>sold event"]
  Q -->|"no"| RF["Buyer gets the full deposit back,<br/>seller gets nothing"]
  RF --> V{"Does the market still own the token?<br/>(nft_token view)"}
  V -->|"yes"| RES["Listing is restored as it was"]
  V -->|"no"| GONE["Listing stays removed,<br/>delisted event: token_left_market"]
  V -->|"view fails"| RES
```

Known limits, from the contract's own README: a Snacc that died while listed cannot be moved by the Snacc contract at all, so its listing stays visible but cannot be bought or delisted, and every buy is refunded in full (only gas is spent). Listed Snaccs keep aging, so check a Snacc with `nft_token` and `get_strain` before you buy. The market is not audited and has unit tests only.

## Events and views

Events use the standard name `snacc_market` (version `1.0.0`): `listed`, `sold`, `delisted` (with `reason` `seller` or `token_left_market`) and `price_updated`. The NFT move itself is also logged by the Snacc contract as a `nep171` `nft_transfer` event.

Views: `get_listing(token_id)`, `get_listings(from_index, limit)`, `get_listings_by_seller(seller_id, from_index, limit)`, `get_listing_count()` and `get_config()` (the NFT contract, the owner, whether listings are paused, `fee_bps` which is 0, and the caps). Paging defaults to 50 and is at most 100.

## In the app

The toolbar `market` button opens the Market window: browse, sell and my listings. The app attaches 100 TGas to a listing call and 150 TGas to `buy` and `delist`. Market calls attach NEAR (a deposit or 1 yoctoNEAR), so they always open the wallet. One-tap play never signs them (see [One-tap play](/docs/one-tap)).

A market refusal is not a failed transaction: a rejected listing hands the Snacc back, and a purchase that cannot settle refunds the buyer, both inside a transaction the wallet reports as successful. For that reason the app reads the chain again after a success and only says "listed" or "bought" when the views agree.
